Production security rules

  • Unit registration requires verification and approval by an authorized platform administrator.
  • Each jail's users, documents, uploads and audit records must be isolated by unit.
  • Unit administrators can manage only their own jail profile and authorized personnel.
  • Central templates may be distributed system-wide, while unit-specific branding remains separate.
  • Real deployment requires an approved server, secure authentication, server-side permissions, encrypted storage and daily backups.
